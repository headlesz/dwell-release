## what dwell is, and isn't

dwell reads and completes reminders through EventKit and shows one of them under the notch.
To know which desktop you are on it uses a private macOS symbol that Apple does not document
or promise to keep. **It is sold as is**: a future macOS may change what the notch is, what a
desktop is, or what an app may draw over the menu bar, and dwell may stop working until it is
updated, or for good. That is a risk you take with it, and it is not grounds for a refund past
the fourteen days. It is the same risk every notch app carries, and it is stated here rather
than discovered.

dwell is not a backup of your reminders and never holds a copy of them. Completing a reminder
in dwell completes it in Reminders, everywhere your account syncs.
